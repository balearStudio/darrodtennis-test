Coach's Whiteboard (coach-whiteboard-warm)

Built on the client's own raspberry, which is the one colour the current identity already owns. The metaphor is the coach's whiteboard: drills, arrows and footwork drawn over bright photos. Warm and personal, the way the reviews talk about Dani; the hero headline is a real review, quoted.

“Buen ambiente, profesionalismo y mucha energía.”

White-on-raspberry 4.88:1 (body on the dominant). Ink-on-blush 14.23:1. Diagram strokes are Ink (3.51:1 on raspberry, 14.23:1 on blush) or White on photos; Sky is decoration only. Registry check was run on the dominant Raspberry.

Type

Display: Young Serif (Google Fonts). Body: Hanken Grotesk. A soft, personal serif with a clean sans; friendly without being childish, works in Spanish and English.

Hero and sections

Hero: tall photo left (site-19) with hand-drawn footwork arrows over the players; raspberry block right carries the headline
Programmes as annotated drill diagrams (court sketch + a line of coach commentary)
Metodología as a whiteboard page: the one methodology, step by step (a true sequence)
Team as coach notes: portrait + a line [CONFIRM: roles, bios and the current coach list are unknown, see brief GAPS]
Reviews set as margin notes beside the photos

Signature motion

Scroll-scrubbed: as the hero pins, arrows draw along the players' footwork like a coach sketching the drill (~3s of scroll). Reduced motion: arrows fully drawn.

Imagery

Bright full-colour photos; SVG line-drawn overlays in Ink and white. Kids are identifiable: consent still open (see brief GAPS).

Atlantic Filmstrip (atlantic-duotone-filmstrip)

Ocean blue and a coral warm note: the Canary sky the club plays under all year. Photos are treated as blue duotone contact-sheet frames that turn to full colour when they reach centre, one frame for each day of the year.

White-on-Atlantic 6.94:1. Deep-on-Mist 14.83:1. Deep-on-Coral 6.60:1 (coral is never text on blue, 2.73:1). Hero type sits on a Deep scrim over the strip, so white text holds 6.94:1+ in both frame states.

Type

Display: Big Shoulders Display (Google Fonts), condensed, uppercase. Body: Public Sans. Tall sports-poster letters against a wide horizontal strip.

Hero and sections

Hero: headline stacked at bottom-left, overlapping a horizontal strip of duotone frames that runs off both edges
Programmes as three tall panels of different heights, not equal cards
Metodología: the academy's one-methodology text set large and static, in the Mist band (the strip is the only pinned moment)
Team: duotone portraits, names set horizontally beneath
Booking: Mist surface band with a coral CTA

Signature motion

The one pinned moment, a horizontal scroll through the "year" (on phones: a plain swipeable strip, no pinning): duotone frames slide across and each one blooms to full colour as it crosses centre. Reduced motion: static strip, all frames in full colour.

Imagery

Blue duotone by default, full colour on focus; needs the high-res site images (site-16/18/19/20 are 1600-1920px).
