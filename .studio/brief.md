# Research brief: Darrod Tennis Academy

## 1. Who they are
A tennis (and padel) academy in Maspalomas / Playa del Inglés, Gran Canaria. It teaches all ages and levels, locals and holidaymakers, on clay courts with year-round play. It works with Sun Club Tennis Playa del Inglés. What sets it apart: every coach uses one in-house methodology, players rotate between coaches, and there is video analysis. A small tournament pathway (ITF juniors, seniors, M15/W25) is in the area. Registered as an SL in July 2020.

## 2. Services & offer
- Junior Tennis, Tennis Pro
- Private and group tennis lessons, private and group padel lessons
- Adult holiday groups
- Tournament coaching support
- Premium hotel service (extra cost)
- Court rental; Facebook also lists a heated pool, bar and pro shop
- Prices per hour: 1 player 60 €, 2 players 80 €, 3 players 100 €, 4 players 120 €

## 3. Reputation
Google: **5.0 from 174 reviews** (only 5 retrievable via the API). TripAdvisor: 5.0. Facebook: 100% recommend from 5 reviews. Instagram: 1,029 followers, 787 posts. Facebook: 494 followers.

Customers praise the head coach Daniel ("Dani"), the friendly and professional team, fast improvement, the chance to hit with players of similar level, and holidaymakers feeling welcome. No complaints appear in the retrieved reviews (sample is small).

## 4. Tone of voice
Warm, energetic, personal, community-minded, professional but informal (tú). Bilingual, and the owner's Instagram mixes Spanish and English. Copy should sound like a friendly coach, not a corporate sports brand.
